FAQ

Questions serious buyers usually ask.

How is GeoLift different from doing this ourselves?
You can manage your profiles internally. GeoLift is useful when ongoing upkeep becomes inconsistent because nobody owns it as a dedicated function. We run the agreed profiles against a repeatable checklist and add a quality-control step before work is considered complete.
What if we only have 1–2 locations?
That's fine. We can support established single-location businesses as well as larger networks. The scope simply becomes smaller.
How fast is onboarding?
The exact timeline depends on access, number of locations and scope. We will confirm the onboarding timeline when the proposal is scoped rather than promising a number before seeing the account.
Do you guarantee ranking improvements?
No. Google determines local rankings through its own systems and algorithms. GeoLift focuses on accurate profile management, consistency, activity, monitoring and a documented operating process. No ranking guarantee is offered.
Will our agency clients know GeoLift is involved?
White-label fulfillment is designed to operate behind your agency. Client-facing delivery and reporting can follow your agreed brand and process. NDA arrangements can also be discussed.
Can we cancel?
Cancellation terms depend on the final service agreement. We will state the applicable notice period and terms clearly in the proposal before work begins.
