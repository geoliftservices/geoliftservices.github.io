Meet the specialist

Dr. Arindam Das

MS (ENT) • Consultant Otologist & Cochlear Implant Surgeon

Dr. Arindam Das is a Consultant Otologist & Cochlear Implant Surgeon with more than 12 years of ENT experience. His professional profile highlights advanced ear care, endoscopic and microscopic ear surgery, cochlear implantation, facial nerve surgery and lateral skull-base surgery. He has mentored 100+ cochlear implantation surgeries with an experienced multidisciplinary team and has published research in international PubMed-indexed journals. He is an Associate Professor at the Institute of Otorhinolaryngology & Head-Neck Surgery, IPGME&R & SSKM Hospital.
